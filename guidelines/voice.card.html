Tone
  • Warm, empathetic, hopeful — speaks to a patient at a difficult moment, not a clinician.
  • Empowerment-oriented: helps the reader ask better questions, not sell a decision.
  • Plain language over clinical jargon.
Examples
✅"Every liver cancer journey is unique, and every conversation has the potential to reveal something new."
❌Naming a specific device, brand, or manufacturer anywhere in the content (this system is intentionally unbranded — see README.md).
❌Any efficacy, safety, or comparative claim — this is educational, not promotional, copy.
Guardrails
  • No PHI (names, identifiable case detail).
  • No device names, logos, or brand accent colors — unbranded except the sponsorship disclaimer.
  • No claims of any kind — this system carries layout and voice guidance only. Real copy needs MLR review before publishing.
